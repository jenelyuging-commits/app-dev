# 🧙‍♀️ Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

## 🌟 Overview
*Frieren: Beyond Journey's End* (*Sōsō no Furīren*) is a contemplative, character-driven fantasy series that explores life *after* the hero's epic journey.

> "The true journey is the one where you stop to truly appreciate the fleeting moments."

## 📜 The Core Concept (Blockquote)

> The Elven mage Frieren was a member of the Hero Party that defeated the Demon King. However, due to her near-immortality, her ten-year adventure was merely a fleeting moment. After her comrade Himmel's death decades later, Frieren is confronted with her own guilt and begins a new, decades-long journey to **learn more about humanity** and the connections she once took for granted.

## 👥 The Current Party (Unordered List)

The current traveling party includes:

* **Frieren:** An elven mage, over one thousand years old, with a passion for collecting peculiar "useless" magic.
* **Fern:** Frieren's quiet, powerful, and pragmatic human apprentice mage.
* **Stark:** A young, outwardly cowardly but inwardly strong human warrior, trained by the dwarf Eisen.
